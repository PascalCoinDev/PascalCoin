PascalCoin for macOS

Install
1. Drag PascalCoin Wallet onto the Applications folder in this window.
   PascalCoin Wallet Experimental is the newer interface; install it too if you
   want to try it.
2. Eject this disk and open the wallet from Applications or Launchpad.

First start
These apps are not notarized by Apple, so macOS asks for confirmation once.
- macOS 15 or later: when the warning appears, click Done. Open System Settings,
  choose Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and click Open Anyway next to
  the PascalCoin app. Confirm with your password.
- macOS 14 or earlier: Control-click the app in Applications, choose Open, then
  click Open in the dialog.

The apps run natively on Macs with Apple silicon (M1 and later) and on Intel
Macs.

Your data
Both wallets keep their keys and blockchain data in the PascalCoin folder in
your home folder. Back up your wallet keys from the wallet before reinstalling
or moving to another computer.

The daemon and reference miner command-line programs are in the separate
x86_64-darwin.tar.gz release package. They are Intel builds, so on Apple
silicon macOS runs them through Rosetta.
